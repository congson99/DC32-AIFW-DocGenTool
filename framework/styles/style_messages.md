# Style: Messages

Applies to `messages_<slug>.md` documents.

---

## Output Format

```md
## 9. Messages

| **Case** | **Message Type** | **Source** | **UI Display** | **Message** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Case] | [Message Type] | [Source] | [UI Display] | [Message] |
```

---

## Section Heading

- Always use `## 9. Messages` as the top-level heading.

---

## Table Formatting

- Use a single table for all messages.
- Keep empty cells blank — do not fill with `-`.
- Do not repeat the header row.
- One row per message case.
